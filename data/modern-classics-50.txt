UK TOP 50 MODERN CLASSIC MOTORCYCLES (BEST VALUE USED BUYING GUIDE)
========================================================================

A comprehensive list of 50 modern classic, retro, and neo-retro motorcycles 
that offer exceptional value, iconic styling, and mechanical dependability on 
the UK secondhand market. 

Categorised by style and displacement for easy browsing.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. ROADSTERS & TRADITIONAL BRITISH RETROS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Royal Enfield Interceptor 650: The UK's best-selling retro; air/oil-cooled twin that is cheap to buy, simple to service, and customisable.
* Triumph Bonneville T100 (Air-Cooled): Pre-2016 865cc models represent peak analog charm with bulletproof mechanicals and slow depreciation.
* Triumph Thruxton 900 (EFI): Classic cafe racer silhouette using the reliable Bonneville platform; holds its price tenaciously.
* Royal Enfield Classic 350: Slow, charming, and hyper-economical roadster that costs pennies to run and insure.
* Triumph Bonneville T120: High-torque 1200cc liquid-cooled machine offering effortless cruising and premium classic finishes.
* Kawasaki W800: Features a beautiful, unique bevel-gear driven camshaft; an authentic Japanese tribute to classic British twins.
* Royal Enfield Bullet 500: The definitive vintage thump; simple single-cylinder engineering that offers a pure, slow-paced riding experience.
* Triumph Speed Twin 900: Formerly the Street Twin; highly accessible low seat height combined with a punchy, modern liquid-cooled motor.
* Moto Guzzi V7 II / III: Transverse V-twin with shaft drive; eliminates chain maintenance entirely and oozes Italian character.
* Mash Black Seven 125: Budget-friendly learner legal retro that offers vintage styling at a fraction of the cost of premium brands.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. JAPANESE UJM, BIG MUSCLE & AIR-COOLED NAKEDS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Yamaha XJR1300: A muscular, air-cooled giant known for an unkillable motor, classic twin shocks, and brute kerbside presence.
* Honda CB1300: Impeccable Japanese build quality with liquid-cooled muscle; early 2000s models are absolute bargains for their build.
* Suzuki GSX1400: Massive torque champion of the muscle bike era; comfortable, physically imposing, and mechanically robust.
* Kawasaki ZRX1200R: Features iconic Eddie Lawson replica styling with a distinctive square headlight fairing and a bulletproof engine.
* Honda CB1100 EX / RS: Air-cooled retro inline-four built with exquisite attention to detail; an investment-grade modern classic.
* Suzuki Bandit 1200 (MK1/MK2): Raw, oil-cooled brute that offers unmatched muscle performance per pound on the UK used market.
* Yamaha XJR400: Rare Japanese grey import that brings classic 90s muscle bike styling down to an accessible, high-revving 400cc class.
* Honda CB750 Seven Fifty: Unfussy, unburstable 90s retro commuter utilizing a low-maintenance hydraulic valve lifter engine design.
* Kawasaki Zephyr 750 / 1100: Early 1990s pioneers of the factory retro movement; highly collectible but still usable daily.
* Suzuki Inazuma 750: An understated retro naked featuring the legendary oil-cooled GSX-R derived motor wrapped in classic lines.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. NEO-RETRO & SCRAMBLERS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Yamaha XSR700: Combines timeless retro bodywork with the award-winning, ultra-reliable MT-07 parallel twin mechanical layout.
* Triumph Scrambler 900 (Air-Cooled): High-level twin exhausts and rugged looks that hold incredible residual value in the UK.
* Ducati Scrambler Icon (800cc): Accessible, air-cooled Desmodromic V-twin that brings premium Italian style to the budget used market.
* Yamaha XSR900 (Gen 1): Blends 1980s racing heritage styling with a ballistic, characterful three-cylinder CP3 powerplant.
* Honda CB650R: Neo-Sports Cafe design featuring gorgeous cascading exhaust headers and a smooth, reliable inline-four engine.
* Royal Enfield Scram 411: An urban, stripped-back scrambler sibling to the Himalayan; rugged, simple, and highly cost-effective.
* BMW R nineT Pure: Stripped-back variant of the iconic air-cooled boxer twin; offers high-end German engineering at a lower entry price.
* Benelli Leoncino 500: Italian-designed, Chinese-built parallel twin scrambler that offers great A2-legal value for money.
* Husqvarna Vitpilen 401: Quirky, ultra-modern cafe racer utilizing KTM 390 mechanicals for a punchy, lightweight ride.
* Caballero Scrambler 500: Fantic's lightweight, punchy single-cylinder scrambler offering high-spec components and premium retro looks.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. CAFE RACERS & RETRO SPORTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Kawasaki Z900RS: A stunning tribute to the legendary 1970s Z1; universally praised for balancing modern performance with true retro DNA.
* Suzuki SV650X: Cafe racer variant of the legendary SV650, offering clip-on handlebars and a bikini fairing on a budget twin platform.
* Honda Hawk 11: A unique, modern cafe racer featuring a rocket fairing and the reliable Africa Twin 1100cc parallel twin motor.
* Triumph Thruxton 1200 R: Premium cafe racer spec with Ohlins suspension and Brembo brakes; an instant classic that retains high value.
* Royal Enfield Continental GT 650: Authentic clip-on cafe racer aesthetics built on an incredibly affordable, low-maintenance engine chassis.
* Suzuki Katana (Modern): Polarising, sharp 1980s throwback styling based on the legendary GSX-S1000 super-naked mechanical platform.
* BMW R nineT Racer: Features a stunning retro half-fairing and low clip-ons; a beautiful, highly collectible investment bike.
* FB Mondial HPS 125: Strikingly styled learner-legal cafe racer that looks like a high-end custom machine for minimal money.
* Herald Cafe 125: Accessible entry point into retro biking for British learners, utilizing simple, easy-to-fix single-cylinder tech.
* Norton Commando 961 Sport: British boutique muscle; higher maintenance but offers unparalleled exclusivity and raw retro character.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. RETRO ADVENTURE, TOURING & CRUISERS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Moto Guzzi V85 TT: Classic classic-transverse V-twin adventure tourer; beautiful retro paint schemes combined with shaft-drive utility.
* Royal Enfield Himalayan 411 (Air-Cooled): Unstoppable budget overland explorer; incredibly simple to repair with basic hand tools.
* Triumph Bonneville Speedmaster: Low-slung, feet-forward retro cruiser built around the highly dependable 1200cc high-torque motor.
* Honda CMX500 Rebel: Contemporary bobber cruiser using the ultra-reliable, highly economical CB500 engine platform.
* Yamaha XV950 Bolt: Japanese reliability meets air-cooled V-twin cruiser styling; a trouble-free alternative to American cruisers.
* Kawasaki Vulcan S: Utilises the proven Ninja 650 parallel twin engine in a cruiser chassis, ensuring cheap parts and easy servicing.
* Triumph Scrambler 1200 XC: High-spec dual-purpose classic capable of genuine off-road riding while maintaining 1960s desert sled style.
* Suzuki VanVan 200: Quirky, fat-tyred retro utility bike; holds near-cult classic status in the UK due to its charm and low running costs.
* Royal Enfield Super Meteor 650: A heavy, stable, premium highway cruiser powered by the award-winning 650cc twin engine platform.
* Chang Jiang Pekin Express: Unique retro sidecar outfit utilizing modern, reliable engine copies for vintage military-style touring.
